⚖️ 3. Comparatives & Superlatives

Remember! kind → kinder → the kindest | clever → cleverer → the cleverest | good → better → the best.
We use than for comparisons: “Mrs Phelps is kinder than Mr Wormwood.” These are our opinions about the characters.

⏰ 4. Past Simple

Remember! go → went • read → read (pronounced “red”) • buy → bought • ask → asked • visit → visited. We use Past Simple for finished actions.

🎭 5. At the Library: Matilda & Mrs Phelps

Read and act out the dialogue. This is a simplified classroom dialogue inspired by Chapter 1, not an exact quotation.

Matilda: Hello, Mrs Phelps!
Mrs Phelps: Hello, Matilda! Do you want a new book?
Matilda: Yes, please. I love reading!
Mrs Phelps: What kind of books do you like?
Matilda: I like interesting stories. Have you got a good book?
Mrs Phelps: Yes! How about The Secret Garden?
Matilda: I read that book. Have you got another one?
Mrs Phelps: You can try Great Expectations by Charles Dickens.
Matilda: Is it more difficult?
Mrs Phelps: Yes, but I think you will enjoy it.
Matilda: Great! Thank you, Mrs Phelps!
Mrs Phelps: You're welcome. Happy reading!

Role-play: Student A = Matilda. Student B = Mrs Phelps. Change the book and make a new dialogue.
